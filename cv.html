Research profile

I study flow, transport, and interfaces to understand and improve cardiovascular medical devices. My background combines computational fluid dynamics, blood-pump and ECMO modeling, and coupled transport with surface reactions at fluid–solid interfaces.

Research interests: cardiovascular flows and device design; advection–diffusion and surface adsorption; multiphase flow, heat transfer, and numerical methods.
